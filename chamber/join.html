NP Membership benefits

No membership fee for qualifying nonprofit organizations.

  • Access to member networking events
  • Nonprofit training and resource sessions
  • Organization listing in the member directory

Bronze Membership benefits

Example annual fee: R$ 300.

  • All NP-level directory and networking benefits
  • Discounted admission to chamber events
  • Access to introductory business workshops

Silver Membership benefits

Example annual fee: R$ 600.

  • All Bronze-level benefits
  • Priority discounts on chamber training
  • One business spotlight on the chamber website each year

Gold Membership benefits

Example annual fee: R$ 1,000.

  • All Silver-level benefits
  • Featured placement in a chamber website spotlight
  • Invitations to exclusive networking and leadership events
  • Additional discounts on event sponsorships
